The Beauty of Typography

Typography is the art and technique of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and visually appealing. Good typography creates balance and harmony in a design.

The first printed books used serif fonts for a classical look, while modern websites often prefer sans-serif fonts for their clean appearance. Both have their place depending on the tone and context of the content.

Designers use line-height, letter-spacing, and font-weight to ensure that the text feels comfortable to read across different devices.
